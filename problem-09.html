Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their college ID card at all times. Entry into the campus without an ID card is not allowed.

Students must follow the college dress code on all working days. This helps maintain a professional and disciplined environment.

Attendance

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in every subject to be eligible to appear for the final exams.

Students with attendance below 75% may be issued a warning.
Attendance below 60% will lead to a fine of Rs. 500.
