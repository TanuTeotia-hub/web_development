About the University

GLA University was established in 2024 and is known for its strong academic programs and modern infrastructure. The university is recognized by the national education board and has produced thousands of successful graduates over the years.

Courses Offered

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs including Computer Science Engineering (AI and ML), Electronics, Mechanical Engineering. Each program is designed with a mix of theory and practical learning.
