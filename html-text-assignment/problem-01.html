GLA University

About the College

GLA University is a well-known engineering college located in Greater noida. It was established in 2024 but it is known for its good faculty, modern labs, and strong focus on practical learning. The college offers a friendly environment for students to grow both academically and personally.

Courses Offered

The college offers several undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs such as Computer Science Engineering, Electronics and Communication, Mechanical Engineering, and Civil Engineering. I am currently studying in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (CSE - AIML).

Campus Life

Campus life at my college is short but full of expierences. There are regular technical events, coding clubs and cultural festivals throughout the year. The campus also has a library, computer labs, cafeteria. Sometimes college feels boring and tired but i enjoy learning new things and becoming more independent. Even though my campus is short, i hope to make good memories here.

Why I Like My College

I like my college because it gives first-year students like me the chance to learn new subjects such as Introduction to Frontend Engineering in a simple and practical way. The supportive teachers and helpful seniors make it easier to understand new topics, and the overall environment motivates me to keep learning.
