About the University

GLA University, Greater Noida is a UGC-recognized institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, management and computer applications. The university is known for its modern infrastructure, experienced faculty and strong industry connections.

Our mission is to provide quality, affordable education to students from all backgrounds while preparing them for successful careers.

Programs Offered

Undergraduate Programs

B.Tech (CSE) - A 4-year program focused on programming, data structures, and modern software development practices.

BBA - A 3-year program covering business fundamentals, marketing and management principles.

BCA - A 3-year program designed for students interested in software development and computer applications.

Postgraduate Programs

MBA - A 2-year program for graduates seeking advanced management skills and leadership training.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for undergraduate programs must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board with the minimum required percentage. Postgraduate applicants must hold a relevant bachelor's degree with minimum eligible marks.

Application Process

  1. Fill the online application form
  2. Upload required documents (mark sheets, photo, signature)
  3. Pay the application fee
  4. Appear for the entrance test or merit evaluation
  5. Check the merit list on the admissions page
  6. Complete admission formalities and fee payment

Key Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualification required to apply for a program.
Merit List
A ranked list of candidates prepared on the basis of entrance test or academic scores.
Scholarship
Financial assistance given to eligible students based on merit or need.
Application Fee
A non-refundable amount paid at the time of submitting the application form.

Scholarships

GLA University offers merit-based scholarships to students who score above the specified percentage in their qualifying examination. Sibling and defence quota concessions are also available. Visit the Admissions section for more details.
