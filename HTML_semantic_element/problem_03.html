About the Clinic

CareWell Multi-Specialty Clinic has been serving the local community for over 15 years, offering consultation and diagnostic services across several departments. Our team of experienced specialists works together to provide accessible, patient-friendly healthcare.

We offer both in-person and online consultations to make healthcare more convenient for our patients.

How to Book an Appointment

  1. Choose the department and doctor
  2. Select your preferred date and time
  3. Fill in your patient details
  4. Submit the appointment request
  5. Receive confirmation via email or SMS
  6. Visit the clinic at the scheduled time with a valid ID proof

Key Terms

Consultation
A scheduled meeting with a specialist to discuss health concerns.
Follow-up
A subsequent visit to review progress after an earlier consultation.
OPD
Out-Patient Department, where patients are seen without hospital admission.
Appointment
A confirmed time slot reserved with a doctor for a consultation.
