Event Overview

The 5th National Career & Education Fair brings together students, job seekers and leading organizations across sectors for two days of placements, internships, workshops and scholarship guidance. This is a free-to-attend event, open to students, graduates and career changers alike.

This is also a paperless registration drive, supporting our CO2-neutral event initiative - all passes, schedules and certificates are issued digitally.

Objectives

The fair aims to bridge the gap between education and employment by giving attendees direct access to recruiters, mentors and higher-education counselors. It also aims to raise awareness about scholarships and apprenticeship programs that are often overlooked by first-generation job seekers.

"Every year, this fair opens doors that many students didn't know existed. Watching a first-time attendee walk out with an internship offer is why we keep organizing this event." - Fair Organizing Committee

Venue

Grand Convocation Center, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The venue is well-connected by metro and offers on-site parking for attendees.

Preparation Guide

Follow the attendee journey below to make the most of your visit, from registration to following up with recruiters afterward.

  1. Register online for the fair
  2. Receive a confirmation email with your registration ID
  3. Download your digital e-pass / QR code
  4. Attend the optional pre-fair preparation webinar
  5. Arrive at the venue and complete on-site check-in
  6. Visit organization booths and submit your resume
  7. Attend scheduled sessions and workshops
  8. Follow up with recruiters after the fair

Preparation Tips & Notices

  • Bring at least 5 printed copies of your resume
  • Dress professionally, as if attending a formal interview
  • Research participating organizations beforehand
  • Prepare a short elevator pitch about yourself
  • Carry a valid photo ID and your digital e-pass
  • Keep your registration QR code handy for quick check-in

Notice: Entry without a valid registration confirmation will not be permitted. Please carry a printed or digital copy of your e-pass on the day of the fair.

Information / FAQs

Common terms used throughout this portal are defined below.

Career Fair
An event where multiple organizations meet prospective candidates for hiring, internships and networking.
Internship
A short-term, often supervised work opportunity that provides practical experience, typically for students.
Placement
A confirmed full-time job offer extended to a candidate by a participating organization.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to students based on merit, need or a specific eligibility criterion.
Apprenticeship
A structured, paid training program that combines on-the-job learning with classroom instruction.

For broader career guidance, you may also refer to the National Career Service or the National Skill Development Corporation.
