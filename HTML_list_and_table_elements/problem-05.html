GLA University Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026 of GLA University, Greater Noida. The three-day fest brings students together to celebrate creativity, talent, technology, culture and teamwork.

Fest Introduction

GLA University Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day student event featuring technical, cultural, creative and academic competitions. Students can participate individually or as teams and showcase their skills.

Registration Process

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

  1. Visit the GLA University fest registration page.
  2. Choose the competition you want to participate in.
  3. Fill in your personal and college details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry their valid GLA University ID card.
  • Participants must report before the scheduled event time.
  • Students must follow the instructions given by coordinators.
  • Participants must maintain discipline on the campus.
  • Any unfair means may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must take care of university property.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible students from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together.
Individual Event
An event where a single participant competes.
Registration
The process of officially signing up for an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last round in which selected participants compete for the final result.
