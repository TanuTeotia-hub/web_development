Royal Palace

Royal Palace is a five-star dining destination in Khurja, Bulandshahr, known for its warm hospitality and a menu that blends classic North Indian flavours with modern presentation. This digital menu covers our Starters, Main Course, Desserts and Beverages, along with our Chef's Recommended Dining Experience.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Chef's Special. Chargrilled cottage cheese marinated in spiced yogurt.
  • Chicken Seekh Kebab - Minced chicken skewers grilled over charcoal.
  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crisp rolls filled with seasonal vegetables.

Main Course

  • Chicken Biryani - our Bestseller, layered basmati rice slow-cooked with tender chicken and whole spices.
  • Dal Makhani - Black lentils simmered overnight with butter and cream.
  • Paneer Butter Masala - Cottage cheese cubes in a rich tomato and butter gravy.

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - our Bestseller, soft milk dumplings soaked in rose-scented sugar syrup.
  • Fresh Fruit Chaat - A light, seasonal fruit medley.

Beverages

  • Masala Chai - Traditional spiced tea.
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Combo offer: free with any Main Course order this weekend

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Begin with a welcome drink of Fresh Lime Soda.
  2. Move on to Paneer Tikka to open the appetite.
  3. Follow with the signature Chicken Biryani as the main course.
  4. Finish with warm Gulab Jamun.
  5. Close the meal with a cup of Masala Chai.

Menu Terms

Vegan
Contains no animal products, including dairy, eggs or honey.
Chef's Special
A dish personally recommended by our head chef for its preparation or flavour.
Gluten-Free
Prepared without wheat, barley or rye-based ingredients.
Bestseller
One of the most frequently ordered dishes on our menu.
Combo
A discounted pairing of two or more menu items sold together.
