1. Introduction

Welcome to the Department Academic Portal of GLA University, Greater Noida.

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering focuses on developing technical knowledge, programming skills and problem-solving abilities among students.

Our academic environment combines theoretical learning with practical and project-based learning.

Students are encouraged to develop skills in programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, databases and software development.

Important Academic Notice: Students should regularly check the academic portal and departmental notices for timetable changes, examinations, assignments and practical schedules.

"Learning by doing helps students convert classroom knowledge into practical skills and real-world solutions."

5. Sequence Before a Practical Class

Students should follow the sequence given below before entering a practical class:

  1. Check the practical timetable.
  2. Read the experiment instructions.
  3. Complete the required theoretical preparation.
  4. Bring the practical notebook.
  5. Reach the laboratory on time.
  6. Follow the instructions of the faculty or lab instructor.

6. Academic Rules

Classroom Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must reach the classroom on time.
  • Students should maintain classroom discipline.
  • Mobile phones should be kept silent during lectures.
  • Students should submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Students must maintain academic integrity.

Important: Attendance, internal assessments and practical records should be maintained throughout the semester.

7. Academic Terminology

Lecture
A classroom session focused mainly on theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming activities.
Tutorial
A guided academic session for solving problems and clarifying concepts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity where students apply theoretical knowledge.
