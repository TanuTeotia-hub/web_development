Admission Overview

GLA University, Greater Noida is an industry-integrated campus of GLA University offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and management. The university holds NAAC A+ accreditation and is recognized by the UGC. This handbook explains the admission process, eligibility rules, required documents, and fee structure for the upcoming academic year.

Eligibility

A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics is mandatory for B.Tech programs. A minimum of 60% aggregate marks in graduation is mandatory for MBA admission. Candidates may qualify through JEE Main, MAT, CAT, XAT or the university's own GLAET entrance test.

Application Process

Admissions are open for the 2026 academic session. Apply before 31st August to be considered in the first counselling round.

  1. Register on the official GLA University admission portal.
  2. Fill in the online application form with personal and academic details.
  3. Upload the required documents.
  4. Pay the application and entrance test fee.
  5. Appear for GLAET, JEE Main, or the relevant entrance exam.
  6. Check the merit list and attend counselling.
  7. Confirm admission through registration and fee payment.

Required Documents

  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets are mandatory
  • Transfer certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Valid government-issued ID proof is mandatory
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Entrance exam scorecard (JEE Main, GLAET, MAT, CAT or XAT), if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualifications a candidate must have to apply for a program.
Merit List
A ranked list of applicants based on their academic performance or entrance test scores such as GLAET or JEE Main.
Counselling
A guided session where shortlisted candidates choose their program and confirm seat allotment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to eligible students based on merit or entrance exam percentile.
Registration
The final step where an admitted student pays fees and formally enrolls at the university.
