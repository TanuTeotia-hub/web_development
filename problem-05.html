Science Notes

Chemistry

A chemical formula shows the exact number of atoms of each element present in a molecule. Below are some common chemical formulas:

Water = H2O

Carbon Dioxide = CO2

Oxygen Gas = O2

Sulphuric Acid = H2SO4

Ammonia = NH3

Chemical bonding is the process by which atoms combine to form molecules, and it is one of the most important concepts in chemistry.

Physics

Force is defined as mass multiplied by acceleration. Some important physics expressions are given below:

Newton's Second Law: F = m.a

Area is measured in square meters, written as m2

Volume is measured in cubic meters, written as m3

Einstein's mass-energy equation: E = mc2, where c represents the speed of light.

Biology

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their own food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide.

During respiration, glucose (C6H12O6) reacts with oxygen to release energy, carbon dioxide, and water.
