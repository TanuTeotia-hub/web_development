City Announces New Metro Line to Reduce Traffic Congestion

Construction to begin next month, officials say

The city transport department has officially approved a new metro line that will connect the eastern and western parts of the city. Officials say the project is expected to reduce daily traffic congestion by a significant margin once completed.

According to the transport minister, the new metro line will cover a distance of 28 kilometers and will include eighteen stations along the route. This is being called one of the most important infrastructure projects in the city's recent history.

Construction work is set to begin on 15th September 2026 near Central Station, and the first phase is expected to be completed within two years. Officials have urged commuters to be patient during the construction period, as some roads may face temporary closures.

Local School Wins National Science Competition

Students recognized for innovative water-saving project

A team of students from Angels Public School has won first place at the National Science Competition held this week. Their project focused on a low-cost method to save water in homes and schools.

The winning team consisted of four students from Grade 11, who worked on the project for over three months under the guidance of their science teacher. Their model impressed judges with its simplicity and real-world usefulness.

The competition was held in New Delhi on 20th August 2026, with participants from over fifty schools across the country. The school's principal said this achievement reflects the hard work and creativity of the students, and hopes it will inspire more students to take interest in science.
