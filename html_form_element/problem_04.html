Employee Leave Request Form

GLA University, Greater Noida allows employees to submit leave requests online through this form. Please fill in all required details accurately. Once submitted, your request will be reviewed by the HR department.

For complete leave policy details, refer to the HR Leave Policy Page.

Leave Categories

Example Leave Categories and Descriptions
Leave Type Description
Casual Leave Short leave for personal reasons, usually 1-3 days.
Sick Leave Leave taken due to illness or medical reasons.
Earned Leave Leave accumulated over time and used as needed.
Work From Home Employee works remotely instead of visiting the office.
Other Any leave category not covered above, with reason specified.
